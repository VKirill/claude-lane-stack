# Discussion modes

The discussion stage picks one mode by what is unknown:

1. **Interview** — the goal is clear, details are not: ask one question at a time, each with a proposed answer the owner can accept.
2. **Options** — the way is unclear: show 2–3 options with costs, recommend one, record the choice as an ADR.
3. **Assumptions** — the owner is away or said «decide»: write the assumptions as locked/free, proceed, list them for review.
4. **Research first** — nobody knows: open a spike or RESEARCH.md before deciding.

Every exchange goes into DISCUSSION.md; decisions go into CONTEXT.md as locked, free or deferred.
