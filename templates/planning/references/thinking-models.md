# Thinking models per role

- **Planner** — work backwards from the promise; every piece has a check that would fail without it.
- **Writer** — smallest change that makes the check pass; read before writing; no speculative code.
- **Critic** — assume the change is wrong and look for the input that breaks it.
- **Verifier** — trust receipts, not reports; rerun one check yourself.
- **Debugger** — one hypothesis at a time, closed by evidence.
- **Designer** — real content first, then hierarchy, then decoration.
- **Researcher** — sources over opinions; mark a guess as a guess.
