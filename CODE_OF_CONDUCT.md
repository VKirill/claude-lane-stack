# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want a **calm, professional** space around multi-agent coding. Harassment,
discrimination, and bad-faith pile-ons have no place here.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and concise  
- Assume good intent  
- Critique ideas and PRs, not people  
- No spam, scams, or unsolicited promotion  

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, threats  
- Doxxing or publishing private info  
- Trolling that derails issues/PRs  
- Sharing secrets “as a joke”  

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, edit, close, or ban.  
Report problems privately via [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or the maintainer’s GitHub profile.
